Detailed Description

Type-safe vectors with integrity verification.

Stamps the template in vec_internal.h once per element type. Each stamp produces a struct plus init/free/reserve/push/pop/verify and indexed ensure/set/get/resize/clear, plus seal to recompute the checksum after direct buffer work, under a per-type prefix. Provided stamps:

  • double: WstVecDouble, wst_vec_double_*
  • int: WstVecInt, wst_vec_int_*
  • uint8_t: WstVecU8, wst_vec_u8_*
  • uint32_t: WstVecU32, wst_vec_u32_*`

Every operation verifies length/capacity invariants, canary blocks on both sides of the buffer and a struct checksum, reporting WstVecErr.
