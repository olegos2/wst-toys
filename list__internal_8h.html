◆ WST_LIST_BLOCK_INIT_CAP

#define WST_LIST_BLOCK_INIT_CAP   8

Pointer/block list backend.

Live nodes are linked through next/prev, and free nodes form a singly linked pool. Nodes live in geometrically growing, never-resized blocks, so public node pointers stay valid across insertions and only become stale after list_clear or list_free. The first insertion allocates the first block.

See list_common_internal.h for the stamp macro contract. Capacity of the first block of nodes.
