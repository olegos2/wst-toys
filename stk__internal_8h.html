◆ WstStkErr

enum WstStkErr

Error that may occur while operating on a stack. Should be always handled after every operation.

Enumerator
WST_STK_NO_ERR 
WST_STK_ERR_NOMEM 

Allocation failure, previous contents are kept.

WST_STK_ERR_EMPTY 

Pop from an empty stack.

WST_STK_ERR_CORRUPT 

Tail canary, struct hash, or length/capacity invariant is damaged.

WST_STK_ERR_OVERFLOW 

Requested capacity does not fit into size_t.
